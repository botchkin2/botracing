# Decision — drop Garage 61 from the app

Date: 2026-09-27
From: botkin (stated in chat), recorded by opus
Thread: ../threads/005-roadmap.md

## The call

Botkin will not use Garage 61 for the foreseeable future. The app shows only his own uploaded laps: recorded on the PC, uploaded to the cloud, read on the phone. "Local" meant that. It does not mean the data stays off the cloud.

## Why

The Garage 61 sign-in wall and redirect keep getting in the way. The phone bug fixed in `6362b61` came from it.

## What this means for the app

Hide it first: no Garage 61 sign-in wall, no auth redirect, no source switch. Put the source behind one config so there are no Garage 61 branches scattered through the UI. Delete the proxy and auth code in R4 cleanup, unless R3 keeps it as an adapter. iRacing comes back through `.ibt` in E7.

The source needs a better name than "local" or "LMU". Botkin to pick.
