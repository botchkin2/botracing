Round 3 · the field · dark only

Race playback and the cars around you

Every frame on this page runs on one clock, the same playhead as Compare's cursor. Press play in any transport and all maps, radars and traces move together. Phone and desktop are laid out separately: the phone stacks map, trace and leaderboard in one column and goes full screen in landscape; desktop gets its own Race workspace (D4). The track and the 58-car, 3-class field are synthetic, so shapes and numbers are illustrative; every rule stated is the one to build.

N1

App mark and navigation

The mark replaces the 18 pt placeholder square in the desktop chrome and on the phone Sessions header, and it is the favicon (this page's tab shows N1a). It is built from the system itself: a 4 pt-radius square on the raised surface with a 1 pt control-border edge, white for data, and amber as the one accent, as for the playback cursor. It holds together at 16 pt. The product name stays in plain type next to it wherever a name is needed. N1a is applied throughout this page; say N1b or N1c to swap.

N2 · NAVIGATION WITHOUT A SESSION Session, Compare, Race and Corner all need a selected session. If one of them is tapped or clicked while no session is selected (first launch, a cleared selection, or a deleted session), the app goes to the Sessions screen instead. The tab is not shown as selected, and no empty workspace or empty state is drawn for it. The same applies to deep links that name a tab but no session. The app mark always goes to Sessions.
R1

Race playback on the track map

Race is a new tab next to Laps in a session (phone) and a new workspace between Compare and Corner (desktop). Dots are coloured by class and sized by class (Hypercar largest), so class also reads by size without colour. Your car is white with a white ring; the focused car gets the amber selection ring. While playing, dots move smoothly between the 5 Hz samples; when paused, the clock snaps to the nearest 0.2 s, so every dot sits on a real sample.

R1e · LABELS AT 58 CARS Off, class position, or car number. One label per car, set just outside the track edge, on the outside first and the inside if that's taken. When two would overlap, the one higher in this order wins: the focused car, you, cars within 3 places of you in your class, class leaders, then overall order. Losing labels are dropped, never shrunk or stacked. The label text takes the class colour; yours is white. At 58 cars expect about 12 labels on the phone and 30 on desktop.
R1f · CLASS COLOURS (ADDITION) Series colours, as three new tokens: --class-hy red, --class-p2 blue, --class-gt3 orange. They colour other cars only (dots, radar blocks, the class bar on leaderboard rows) and never a line or a number, so they don't collide with lap colours or with red = slower. GT3 orange is set redder and darker than the amber UI colour, and the amber focus ring sits outside the dot. Classes also differ in dot size. Distinct in Tweaks is a fallback palette if the orange still reads as selection.
R2

Cars around you

Your car is fixed in the centre pointing up. Every car within ±{{ RS.R }} m is drawn to scale at its true 2D position and heading relative to yours (world position from the 5 Hz field data, rotated into your heading), so in a corner the car ahead sits off to the side and turned. Ticks at 10, 20 and 30 m, ahead and behind, carry the distance. Cars fade out over the last 6 m of range instead of popping. A side bar lights when a car's length overlaps yours and it is within 6 m sideways. The radar steps at 5 Hz even while the map moves smoothly, and the time of the sample shown is printed in the corner. Cars in the pit lane are drawn at 40%.

R3

What the distance data tells us: tags

New codes in the style of OUT, IN, OFF, SLOW and HIT: short caps, a number where it helps, the rule in the key. They describe what happened on a lap; none of them excludes a lap except RESET. TOW ranks directly after the exclusion codes, so at 375 pt a towed lap shows TOW even when it's also the best lap.
