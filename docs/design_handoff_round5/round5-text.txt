Decisions
Absent VE removes things. It is never shown as 0 %, “—” or a disabled column: no VE bar, no “RUNS OUT FIRST” box, no VE column in Stops or To drop a stop, no “1 % VE” or Max VE in the rules.
With one meter, the Fuel bar goes to full ink, and the explainer says why there is no comparison (“This car has no VE”).
Session: the stint line is just “Fuel 3.12 L/lap (n = 19)” and the pit note drops the “/ n % VE” part. Same fixed heights as the VE state.
Pit stops: IN and ADDED show litres only. No refuel split for LMP2 until the rate is measured; the pit-lane time stands alone.
Rule: the VE state is chosen per session from whether the channel is stored. Nothing is decided by car class, so a future car with VE works without a code change.

Decisions
Refuel is computed from litres added ÷ 3.4 L/s: +33.2 L → 9.8 s, +50.0 L → 14.7 s. The line prints the rate, so the number is not mistaken for a measurement.
Count-aware. 0 stops: the card becomes “Fuel”, covering start, used and end of the last lap, and the plan half compares it with Load to finish. 1 stop: a single full-width column, so the bars get the whole width. 2 stops: two columns. 3 or more: fixed 118 pt columns that scroll sideways, with the key column pinned.
Stops are columns, so the same measure sits side by side and scans across. Each cell has a bold primary value (litres, seconds) with its secondary value muted below it.
Two small to-scale bars carry the shape: VE out (left + added) and pit lane (refuel inside the lane time). They are neutral inks only, keyed once under the table.
Tyres read “Changed” or “Not changed”. When per-wheel data ships, the same row carries “Fronts new” with no layout change.
The end row is “End of L72 · the last whole lap”.
Plan vs what happened is the lower half of the same card, separated by a stronger rule. It uses the same key column, with Plan in secondary ink and Actual at full ink, row for row: stop laps, what was left at each entry, and the spare.
The plan is rebuilt from the laps recorded before the race, so it is the plan he could have seen that morning. The explainer says so and gives n.
No difference column and no colour. The two values sit side by side and the reader compares them.

Corner fixes: throttle strip, steering, sampling text
‹
T5
38 laps · vs L16
Full throttle by the slowest point: 27 of 38 laps
?
What
Car speed along the corner, one line per lap.
Measured
Game telemetry at 100 Hz, drawn at its recorded samples. Laps are aligned on a 5 m position grid.
Direction
Higher = faster. The lowest point of each line is its minimum speed.
Decisions
Throttle stays a DotStrip of the measurement: metres after the apex to full throttle. Laps already flat at the slowest point drop out, and their count takes one fixed 24 pt line under the strip.
The live line reads “27 laps”. This version keeps the denominator (“27 of 38 laps”) and puts “Laps already flat at the slowest point are not plotted” in the DotStrip explainer, so the missing dots are explained.
Cells stay only for “No brake in this corner”, as in round 4.
Steering: “Steering, % of full lock”, with no direction wording until the sign is measured.
MEASURED lines state each channel’s own rate: pedals 50 Hz, speed and steering 100 Hz, position 10 Hz, 5 m grid.

Decisions
Plan desktop is three columns: inputs on the left (with the last race and the full rules visible), the answer in the middle in the same card order as the phone, and the practice “Use and lap time” chart on the right. The data the plan is built from sits beside it.
With the width available, Stops gains VE per stint and refuel time per stop (litres ÷ 3.4 L/s). Race and Per tank sit side by side, so the answer and the limit read together.
Session desktop: the lap table carries the stint fuel lines and pit note rows at the same fixed heights as the phone. Each pit row links to the card on the right.
The Pit stops card moves to a 400 pt right column, pinned beside the table, so the stops and the laps around them are visible together. The same 0 / 1 / 2 / 3+ rules apply (the demo switch in item 3 drives this frame too).
At 900–1279, the right column drops below the table, as with Stints in D1.
Track page and Compare on desktop are not drawn: Track takes the Plan block per car above its session list, and Compare is D2 unchanged.
