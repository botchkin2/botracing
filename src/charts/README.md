# src/charts

The react-native-svg chart layer: Axis, Trace, Band, Cursor, Bars, DotStrip, CornerGrid (and `CornerCells`, one row of its cells: the phone's lap table draws them too), RaceLanes (the YOUR RACE lanes; the layout is `raceLanesLayout.ts`), FuelScatter (practice fuel use against lap time), SplitBar (a small to-scale bar in two neutral inks, made of plain views: the Pit stops card's VE out, pit lane and used-and-left). Pure props in, SVG out.

- **Text inside the box.** Every chart svg clips at its edges, so a chart is a `ChartSvg` and its text a `ChartLabel` (`ChartLabel.tsx`), in the axis face (the metrics in `labelBox.ts` assume it). A chart places each label from its box: a y tick centred on its value's line, with the plot padded by half a label box (`traceAxis.ts` `plotPad`, `lapBarsPlot.ts`), so the plot shrinks and the tick stays on its line; a tag or x tick hangs under or stands on an edge (`baselineUnder`, `baselineOver`); y tick labels that would overlap are thinned (`labelledTicks`). `ChartLabel` still holds a label inside as a safety net and warns in dev when it has to move one. ESLint (`no-restricted-imports`) refuses `Text`, `TSpan` and `TextPath` from react-native-svg, aliased or not, outside `ChartLabel.tsx` and the maps (BrakeMap, CarDots, FollowMap, TrackMap place labels on the track's own geometry). DotStrip's axis is inside its svg too: x ticks hang under the axis line, the plot is padded by half the widest tick label, and labels that would touch are thinned (`stripAxis.ts`). React Native text laid beside a chart (DotStrip's direction words, the Plan StopSlider's lap labels) is outside the svg and outside this rule. Axis ticks carry their unit (`tickFormat.ts`): `500 m`, `260 km/h`, `−50 %`, `L10`, `+0.5 s`.
- **Follow map world.** `FollowMap` draws road, lines and ticks once on a static Svg of its own (1 unit = 1 m, widths set in metres from the zoom) inside a clipped View; a frame moves that View with translate, rotate and scale. Dots, corner numbers, cars and the scale bar are a screen-space Svg over it.
- **Moving trace lines.** `TraceChart` draws the lines once in their own static Svg (three windows wide) inside an overflow-hidden View; a frame moves that View with `translateX`, never a transform inside the svg (on Android every frame re-rasterised all paths: 2x fewer frames). Bands and grid sit under the lines layer; marks, dim, hover and cursor over it.
