# src/analysis

Pure TypeScript analysis shared with the uploader (`tools/sessions/analyze.mjs` runs it in Node as is).

- Belongs here: consistency, corners, lap classification, window math, time-diff rebase, resampling, the field (`field.ts`), the min and max of any stretch of a long array without rescanning it (`rangeIndex.ts`), its classes (`fieldClasses.ts`: one table per session, the colours and labels every screen uses; a known class keeps its colour, others go by pace), the race clock (`raceClock.ts`: race time and the player's lap and distance) and the YOUR RACE lanes (`raceLanes.ts`: pit, tow and battle spans, blue flags and passes over race time, and the player's off-track spans handed in from `offTrackEvents.ts`; `tools/sessions/raceLanes.parity.test.mjs` keeps its rules equal to the uploader's).
- Also here: the measured track surface (`trackSurface.ts`: centre path and edges from PathLateral and TrackEdge, summed per 10 m bin so sessions add up, and the rule that drops OSM roads inside it).
- Also here: the pre-race fuel and VE planner (`fuelPlan.ts`): numbers from the driver's own green laps for one set of event rules, no advice.
- Also here: the reference-lap ranking (`referenceLap.ts`): which lap is a fair ruler for another (same car and session kind, a similar fuel load, tyres kept, clean air), as matches and numbers.
- Also here: the corner windows (`cornerBoundaries.ts`): an ordered list of boundaries from the line to the line, each section's window a margin before the earliest brake or lift onset seen at the layout, so the windows tile the lap and add up to its time; the corner and exit split and the brake applications of a window. Pure; the uploader will cut section times and slices with it.
- `steps.ts`: work written as a generator that yields between small chunks, run to the end at once (`runSteps`, tests and Node) or in time slices that give the thread back (`runSliced`, a page that must not freeze). `worldRoad.ts` and `raceState.ts` `prepareRaceSteps` are written this way.
- `segments.ts`: the one shape per-section tables read: a `SegmentTimes` (a lap cut into labelled segments, a time per lap) made by two producers in `data/sessions/segments.ts`, our turn sections or the game's S1–S3, picked by the Settings toggle (`state/sectionPrefs.ts`). The bests, medians, spread and optimum all take it and never branch on which producer made it. They count every comparable lap: tow and traffic never remove a time (Botkin, 2026-10-01; they stay flags on laps and cells), as in `windowOptimum.ts`.
- Also here: the optimal lap (`sectionOptimum.ts`): per stint and corner window, the best and median of the times a caller passes, and their two sums, hidden under 5 laps. The caller (`data/sessions/windowOptimum.ts`) decides which window times count: only pit, off-track and local-yellow windows are left out, never tow or traffic. `windowMedians` gives each window's median over a set of laps the caller picked (Compare's checked laps).
- Also here: one car's laps from the field (`carLaps.ts`), on the same lap clock as the class pace (`classLaps.ts` `walkCarLaps`): approximate times, pit laps tagged in/out/pit, "—" where the car left the field.
- `setStats.ts`: the one place a median, a quantile and a spread (IQR, empty under 2 values) are computed, over values a caller picked, with missing values skipped and never read as 0 (decisions/lap/2026-10-11-selection-and-numbers.md: one definition on every screen). `columnStats` (`segments.ts`) and every screen read it; a new median helper is a bug. Analysis files that import each other write the `.ts` extension (`./setStats.ts`): Node runs them as is for the uploader, and `tsconfig.json` allows it. `resample.ts` and the geometry in `trackSurface.ts` keep their own hot-loop medians.
- `paceEvents.ts`: which laps are slow, the one rule for every session size (decisions/lap/2026-10-12-default-selection-outliers.md). A lap is slow when its time is more than 3.5 robust sigmas (small-sample corrected, at least 0.3 s) above its stint's pace curve, a running median of 5 laps of the lap's pace factor, which comes from its sections so a mistake in one or two of them cannot bend it. A session needs 5 candidate laps (timed, whole, no pit lane); below that no lap is slow. `slowLapIds` is the only place it is decided: the uploader stores `reasons: ['slow']` from it (`tools/sessions/analyze.mjs` `markSlow`, run before and after the corner facts) and the app's default selection (`data/sessions/defaultLaps.ts`) leaves the laps with that stored reason unticked: the app computes nothing a second time. Do not add a second slow rule.
- Not here: React, formatting, fetching.
- Imports: **nothing**. Erasable TS syntax only, no path aliases.
