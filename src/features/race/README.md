# src/features/race

The Race screen (round 3, handoff R1): every car on the track as a dot, a leaderboard synced to the playhead, and a clock shared with Compare. In practice and qualifying the same screen is the Field tab (`RaceMode` 'field'): no class places or gaps to a leader, the road gap from you in seconds in their place, and one line on who is ahead, behind and which faster class is coming (`model.ts` `roadSummary`).

- `RaceScreen.tsx`: layout (phone column, desktop `map | leaderboard` from 1280, the leaderboard below the lanes from 900 to 1279), the states of R4c, focus, class filter.
- `chaseWorld.ts`: the 3D's road and cars' preparation as steps (`analysis/steps`), driven in 8 ms time slices by `useRaceData` when the 3D is first asked for (a second or two on a phone, never one frozen block); the map shows a placeholder until it returns, and a new field, map or race drops a fit that is running.
- `useRaceData.ts`: gathers the session, its field, the track map and the reference lap; says whether the cars land on the drawn track (`analysis/worldMatch`).
- `useRaceClock.ts` + `clock.ts`: race time, play/pause, rates; playback steps by wall time, paused rests on a real 5 Hz sample (the numbers and the 2D map; the 3D keeps drawing the interpolated field, so a pause moves nothing in it). In 3D React sees the clock at the field's own rate (`tickHz`, 5 Hz) and the engine draws every frame from its own clock (`analysis/chaseClock`, which takes the app's time only on a seek, play, pause or rate change), so the screen re-renders 5 times a second, not 60.
- `selectionClock.ts`: Compare's cursor (lap + metres in the URL) to race time and back, via `analysis/raceClock`.
- `followTarget.ts`: pure. Which car Follow chases (focused, else you) and where its chase view sits and points, through the map's own projection.
- Places: class places and the printed place go by the game's place with its blips taken out (`analysis/placeBlips`, `prepareRace`'s `placeSteady`): LMU's own place reads wrong for an update or two when a car crosses the start line (lap still 0, distance wrapped) and swaps neighbours for one update at a time (Sebring Oct 21 and 22: 19 and 915 blips in a race). A departure that is back within 3 updates (0.6 s) takes the place it left; a change that holds longer is an overtake and stays. The field's own `place` is untouched (the race result reads it).
- `model.ts`: pure. Cars at a moment (`analysis/raceState`) to leaderboard groups, dots and the focus label.
- `carLapsView.ts`: pure. The focused car's laps (`analysis/carLaps`) as panel rows, approximate (`≈`), newest first; `components/CarLapsPanel` draws them under the car's leaderboard row.
- `chase/`: the 3D chase view (web only, phone web included; lazy-loaded with `three` from `components/ChaseView.web.tsx`, one engine and one WebGL context for the view's life, full screen included). An instrument, nothing the data does not hold: near-black space over a ground that follows the road's height 60 m out from each edge and a flat plane beyond it to the horizon, a faint datum grid, a post every 20 m and ground ticks beside the road (`chase/roadSide`) to read speed and angle against.
  - Engine: runs its own race clock (`analysis/chaseClock`, following the app's on a seek, play, pause or rate) and samples every car each animation frame through the Race screen's `carsAt`.
  - Cars: the field's own x/z through a Hermite curve (`carsAt(..., smooth)`), never re-placed on the surface's line. Each is a lit low-poly body in its class colour (class edges over a dimmed fill); the player has light edges and a ring, and his own inputs are painted on the road with 3/2/1 bars before his brake-ons (from his trace, only where it lies within 20 m of him). Heading: `analysis/chaseHeading` (the stored yaw where it agrees with the road or the car's travel, otherwise the travel, otherwise the road).
  - Road: fitted from the field in the game's own world frame (`analysis/worldRoad`): every moving car sample moved onto the centre line (x/z minus `pathLateralM`), a Gaussian local-linear fit over lap distance, 1.5 m equal arc steps, no bins. Half-widths from the surface's measured edges; height from the track's curated `elevation` (API `/map`) at the point's lap distance. Every car stands on the road at its height and pitches to its grade; no roll (not stored); with no profile the road is flat.
  - Camera: Chase and High are a game's chase cam (`analysis/chaseFollow`): a fixed distance behind the car on a direction smoothed over 0.2 s (shorter at 4x and up) and led by the measured turn rate times that time, so the car stays on the camera axis through a hairpin; looking at the car. Pausing moves nothing: the engine holds its own time (`chaseClock`) and the rig stops easing until the car is moved by a scrub. Drag turns and raises the camera round the car, the wheel zooms, Reset (button or double click) eases back (`analysis/chaseFreeCam`, Chase and High; Heli's drag is its own look-round). TV and Heli ride the road.
  - HUD (`components/ChaseView.web.tsx`): the followed player's own inputs (`DriverInputs`: wheel and pedals) and the numbers (class place and gaps, speed, gear; grade and height on a desktop only; on a phone two rows, place and gear + speed: the strip above already prints each neighbour's gap, so the box does not repeat it, `chase/numbersRows`); a timing tower (`ChaseTower`, rows from the pure `chase/towerRows`): the cars around the followed car by class place (its own class, the HUD's basis) or by road distance, each row labelled with its class tag and class place, a tap on a row follows that car. On a desktop the tower floats over the corner, folded by its ☰. On a phone nothing floats over the canvas but the HUD in its two bottom corners: the top is the app's mode row (`size.chaseControlsH`), then `ChaseNearStrip` (the followed car and its neighbours, one tap to follow; its arrow opens the longer list), then the canvas, then the camera chips. The 3D box is half the screen (`phone3dHeight`). A finger drag in it is the camera's or the page's, decided once from its first 10 px (`analysis/panGesture`): within 35° of the horizontal it turns the camera by its sideways travel only (pitch stays on a mouse drag), steeper it scrolls the app's page. The WebView takes its touches whole (`nestedScrollEnabled`, so the page's ScrollView never steals a camera drag), so in the app the 3D page forwards the page's share as `{t:'pan', dy}`, summed once an animation frame and carried on after the lift as a fling (`panGesture`); the app scrolls its ScrollView by -dy without animation (`pageScroll`, `usePageScroll`). `pointercancel` and `lostpointercapture` end a drag at once. Key (the class legend) is on the 2D maps only.
  - Pure parts in `analysis/`: `chaseHeading`, `worldRoad`, `chaseRoad` (the type and the profile reads), `chaseRoadIndex`, `chaseFollow`, `chaseHud`, `chaseMath`.
- `embed3d.ts`: the 3D on the Android app is the web race page in a WebView (`components/ChaseView.tsx`, react-native-webview: a native module, so an APK): `/session/{id}/race?embed=3d` at the app's selection, signed in by a custom token in the `#ct=` fragment from `POST /api/auth/handoff` (`data/handoff.ts`); the page opens on the 3D, full screen, with no way out and no app chrome around it (`app/_layout` drops the bottom bar for `embed=3d`; `RaceScreen` `embed3d` renders a dark page until the race is ready), and no transport of its own. The app's race clock is the only clock (`embedBridge.ts`, `useEmbedClock.ts`): the app posts a validated `{t:'clock', timeS, playing, rate}` on a seek, play, pause or rate change and once a second, the page applies it and answers one message, `{t:'ready'}`, when its first frame is drawn; the app keeps a dark cover over the WebView until then (and Retry after 30 s). The page's only other message is `{t:'pan', dy}` (finite `dy`, clamped to ±1000 px, `parsePanMessage`). Nothing else crosses, and no token or user data. The WebView may show the hosting origin only (`allowsNavigation`).
- Road bank (the 3D's cross-slope, thread 94 #6338): the track's curated `bank` (API `/map`, `tools/curate plan-bank`, the capture's mOri roll, read by `analysis/bank` `bankOnLap` through `worldRoad`) tilts the road about its centre line (`chase/roadScene`, `roadSide`: the right side rises by its distance x tan(roll)) and every car takes its roll from it and its height at its lateral offset (`analysis/chasePose` `surfacePose`, `chase/carPose` `setCarRotation`); pitch is the road's grade, not a second profile. A track with no curated bank is level across, as before.
- `components/`: `RaceMap` (over `charts/TrackMap`, `charts/FollowMap` in Follow, `ChaseView` in 3D), `Leaderboard`, `RaceTransport`, `RaceLegend`, `RaceLanesBlock`.

Not here: the lane model (`analysis/raceLanes`) and chart (`charts/RaceLanes`), the radar (`charts/Radar`), field decoding (`data/field`).
Imports: anything under `src/` except another feature. Only `app/` routes import this folder.
