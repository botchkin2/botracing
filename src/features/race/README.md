# src/features/race

The Race screen (round 3, handoff R1): every car on the track as a dot, a leaderboard synced to the playhead, and a clock shared with Compare. In practice and qualifying the same screen is the Field tab (`RaceMode` 'field'): no class places or gaps to a leader, the road gap from you in seconds in their place, and one line on who is ahead, behind and which faster class is coming (`model.ts` `roadSummary`).

- `RaceScreen.tsx`: layout (phone column, desktop `map | leaderboard` from 1280, the leaderboard below the lanes from 900 to 1279), the states of R4c, focus, class filter.
- `useRaceData.ts`: gathers the session, its field, the track map and the reference lap; says whether the cars land on the drawn track (`analysis/worldMatch`).
- `useRaceClock.ts` + `clock.ts`: race time, play/pause, rates; playback steps by wall time, paused rests on a real 5 Hz sample.
- `selectionClock.ts`: Compare's cursor (lap + metres in the URL) to race time and back, via `analysis/raceClock`.
- `followTarget.ts`: pure. Which car Follow chases (focused, else you) and where its chase view sits and points, through the map's own projection.
- `model.ts`: pure. Cars at a moment (`analysis/raceState`) to leaderboard groups, dots and the focus label.
- `carLapsView.ts`: pure. The focused car's laps (`analysis/carLaps`) as panel rows, approximate (`≈`), newest first; `components/CarLapsPanel` draws them under the car's leaderboard row.
- `chase/`: the 3D chase view's engine (web only, lazy-loaded with `three` from `components/ChaseView.web.tsx`, one engine and one WebGL context for the view's life, full screen included; phones keep the 2D Follow map). An instrument, nothing the data does not hold: near-black space over a ground that follows the road's height 60 m out from each edge and a flat plane beyond it to the horizon, with a faint datum grid, with a post every 20 m and ground ticks beside the road (`chase/roadSide`, from the road's own edges) to read speed and angle against, the road at its measured width with thin edges and a cross rung every 10 m, every car a lit low-poly body in its class colour (class edges over a dimmed fill), the player marked by light edges and a ring, his own inputs painted on the road with 3/2/1 bars before his brake-ons (from his trace, only where it lies within 20 m of him). The engine runs its own race clock (`analysis/chaseClock`, following the app's on a seek, play, pause or rate) and samples every car each animation frame through the Race screen's `carsAt`, with no smoothing on top, so a car is drawn exactly where the data puts it. Cars are the field's 5 Hz samples interpolated linearly (within 0.2 m and 0.25 degrees of the 100 Hz player stream at p95, thread 83 #6136). Height comes only from the track's curated `elevation` (API `/map`, read by `analysis/elevation` `heightOnLap` through `analysis/chaseRoad`): every car stands on the road at its height and pitches to its grade; no roll (not stored); with no profile the road is flat. Chase and High are a game's chase cam (`analysis/chaseFollow`): a fixed distance behind the car on a direction smoothed over 0.3 s (shorter at 4x and up), looking at the car, so the car stays in the middle of the frame through any bend and its size never changes; TV and Heli ride the road. The road is fitted from the field in the game's own world frame (`analysis/worldRoad`): every moving car sample moved onto the centre line (its x/z minus `pathLateralM`) and a Gaussian local-linear fit over lap distance, 1.5 m equal arc steps, no bins; half-widths from the surface's measured edges, height from the curated profile at the point's lap distance. Cars are the field's own x/z through a Hermite curve (`carsAt(..., smooth)`), never re-placed on the surface's line, so a car sits on the road it drove on (within 0.05 m of the car's own pathLateral at the median). The pure parts live in `analysis/`: `chaseHeading` (which way a car points: the stored yaw where it agrees with the road or the car's travel, otherwise the travel, otherwise the road), `worldRoad`, `chaseRoad` (the type and the profile reads), `chaseRoadIndex`, `chaseFollow`, `chaseHud`, `chaseMath`.
- `components/`: `RaceMap` (over `charts/TrackMap`, `charts/FollowMap` in Follow, `ChaseView` in 3D), `Leaderboard`, `RaceTransport`, `RaceLegend`, `RaceLanesBlock`.

Not here: the lane model (`analysis/raceLanes`) and chart (`charts/RaceLanes`), the radar (`charts/Radar`), field decoding (`data/field`).
Imports: anything under `src/` except another feature. Only `app/` routes import this folder.
