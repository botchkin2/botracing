# src/features/plan

The Plan screen: the stops, laps and fuel for the next race at a track and car, from the driver's own recorded laps, and the class pace of the cars he races with.

- `PlanScreen.tsx`: layout (phone column; desktop setup | results | scatter) and which cards each `PlanState` draws (`planState.ts`).
- Entry for a track he has never driven (M11): the Track page's Plan block shows one card that opens `planHref` for that track in the car of his newest LMU session (`undrivenPlanKey` in `model.ts`). The Plan opens its `undriven` state, where the car picker changes the car.
- The rules form (`RulesEditor.tsx`) has no VE field: the plan takes VE from his sessions of the rules and no series VE % is applied (thread 104); a preset's stored `vePct` stays in its doc and is not read. The page's column is `columnWidth` (content plus both gutters), so the cards and the charts drawn to the content width agree.
- Which track and car the Plan is about (`Combo`, `planCombos`, `defaultCombo`, `resolveCombo`, the chip labels, `PLAN_HISTORY_DAYS`) is `src/workspace/planCombo.ts`, shared with the Track page and the desktop bar (ticket 34). The Plan keeps its pick in the URL's `combo` (`PlanScreen` writes and reads it back), which the bar reads in `src/workspace/usePlanCombo.ts`.
- `usePlanData.ts`: the rules, history, plan and cards for one track and car; the Track page reads the same hook.
- The unit the plan is made for is the **rule set** (`ruleSet.ts`, `planEvent.ts`), not the series week (thread 104): the sessions of a track and car whose litres per 1 % VE spread no more than 0.015, newest first, so a practice that ran last week's rules sits in that week's set. Its **source** is the newest race or qualifying (a practice-only set says so): the ratio, the load (its fill limit, else the newest fill limit in the set, else the newest race's start; never a practice's or a qualifying's start, and never the tank: a live capture reports the car's own 117 L, not the event's cap) and the stop ratio come from it first. The Rules chip names it ("Rules from your offline practice, Oct 26 · One Stint Sprint": the session's type, whether it ran offline or online, and its date; a series week of a car with no VE keeps its load first). The load is no longer in the label: Max fuel is the facts line's. A session with no ratio (a block from before analysis version 11) is in no rule set, so "your last race here" shows the newest race of the planned set. A car with no VE at all keeps series-week events. **No load known** (a rule set of practices only): `PlanRules.fuelL` is null and the plan runs on VE alone: no fuel meter (`planRace` plans no fuel use), no litres anywhere (no start placeholder, no litres-a-lap saving target, no fuel check on the stop), the limit reads "VE limits" and the facts line says "Max fuel: not in this recording"; a car with no VE at all stays `needs-fuel`.
- `model.ts`, `planCards.ts`, `pitPlan.ts`, `planHalf.ts`, `pooledUse.ts`: pure. Combos, the rules line and the basis notes, the Race card, the pit slider, pooled use.
- The page (pit-wall thread 99, row PLN, design #6684, UX note #6729), top to bottom: the per-lap strip (`perLapStrip.ts`: VE per lap first for a VE car, then p90; the basis under it), the facts line under it (`planChecks.ts`, `planChecksText.ts`: "Max fuel 73 L · Fuel 3.57 L a lap · VE 5.7 % a lap", each "checked", "1 source" or "disagrees", and the offline-vs-online note), the Race tiles (laps and the late flag, stops, start load), the Pit plan (`stopPanels.ts`: a panel per stop with its slider and the number to set in the pit menu, from `analysis/stopTarget.ts`; `stintStrip.ts`: the stints as a bar, a tap opens a stint's numbers; the stint table on desktop), Saving (`saving.ts`: the use a lap the plan asks for, his laps at or under it and his best stint median, shown when a slider stop runs dry at p90 or some of his laps already reach one stop fewer), the Load to finish table (desktop, no-stop races), class pace. The car decides the unit: VE for a VE car, litres otherwise; there is no units switch. Every colour is a token: the number he sets is in accent ink (the warning tone when the stint after it runs dry at p90).
- The stop number (`analysis/stopTarget.ts`): LMU fills TO a level, so the stop reads "Set VE 62 %": the laps to the next stop (or the flag, plus the late-flag lap in a timed race) at p90 use, rounded up to the whole % (the menu's step, Botkin #6705). Fuel follows VE at the litres one % adds at his own stops (`stopRatio`, from the rule set's source session first, then the newest race, qualifying, practice that measured one; the litres and seconds of a stop are that, never the load over 100), so the level is the higher of the VE need and the fuel need over what the car arrives with, and the stop is fuel-limited when fuel is more than one step ahead; with no stop measured the stop shows VE only and says "no stop measured". A fuel-only car sets litres; iRacing adds an amount.
- The plan basis (pit-wall #4170): fuel and VE per lap over every green lap (a lap in traffic or a tow burns the same); the lap time and the traffic medians over the comparable green laps only (`analysis/fuelPlan.ts` `paceLaps`; under 3 comparable laps the green laps, and `perLap.lapTimeBasis` says so).
- Lap names (pit-wall thread 99, row FML): racing lap n is L<n>, from the green flag, through `analysis/lapNumbering.ts` `lapLabel`; the formation is its own row, "Formation", with no number. It burns fuel on top of the race laps (`formation.ts`: the measured formation alone, else 0.4 laps) and no VE.
- The pit model (`pitBase.ts`): the refuel rate is the median pump rate measured on his own race stops here (`fillLPerS`, any visit that added fuel), else the GT3 rate (3.4 L/s, thread 34), else none and the time is left out. The lane base is learnt from service visits only. "Your last race" names a repair or a penalty by what it was, never as a stop (`lastRace.ts`).
- Class pace (Botkin's D55): `classTiming.ts` is pure. It pools each session's stored `classLaps` (`analysis/classLaps.ts`) into one row per class: lap (≈, to a tenth), its lap against his median (minus is faster), first catch, every. `classPaceInput.ts` (pure) picks the sessions (same sim and track, races and practices, whatever car), his class and his median. `useClassTiming.ts` only gathers them. `components/ClassTimingSection.tsx` draws the Race timeline and the Class pace card on both widths and in every plan state: class pace needs no fuel and none of his laps.
- Imports `src/analysis`, `src/data`, `src/design`, `src/ui`, `src/state`, `src/charts`, `src/nav`, and `features/session/pitCard` (the pit stop facts the Race card shares).
- The facts line (`planChecks.ts`, pit-wall thread 104 #7391/#7393/#7398-#7401, ticket 47): each number reads "checked" when two sources agree, "1 source" when only one exists, "disagrees" when the second contradicts it; the plan uses its own number either way. **Max fuel** is the setup's `maxValue` (the cap in a capped week, else the tank), its second source another run's `maxValue` of the rule set, or in a capped week its own str x 100; a live capture's tank is no source (it reports the car's 117 L whatever the week). **Fuel a lap** is the plan's median against the median of his other runs here, any rules, within 4 %. **VE a lap** is the plan's median against LMU's own expectation in the setup (`fuel.setupVePerLapPct` from `VM_VIRTUAL_ENERGY`, the file with the highest VE setting), measured 3 % under to 10 % over. The duplicate docs of one run (same track and car, start within 60 s: the tray's capture and the game file) are one run, never their own second source. A session synced before the field existed has no LMU expectation until it re-syncs, so its VE reads "1 source". The offline line says "not yet compared" until an online session sits in the offline practice's rule set (litres per 1 % VE within `RATIO_TOLERANCE`), then "matched".
- The start the plan assumes (`startNote.ts`, pit-wall thread 104 #7941/#7945/#7952-#7958, ticket 47) is said at the top of the Race card on both widths: "Your start 87 %" when he typed it, "Your start 100 % (not set)" otherwise; a VE car with only litres typed still reads "Your start 100 % (not set) · 62 L". Desktop has the Start fields; the phone has one field in the Race card (VE, else litres), because the start decides the stops. What he typed is kept on the device for that track and car (`src/state/planStart.ts`; typing for another combo replaces it), so it survives leaving the Plan. It is never read from a session's setup (the newest earlier session is usually a qualifying at 16-28 %, so a setup default would be worse than a full load). The Race card's third tile is "Start with": what the plan says to put in the garage. No-data words: "No session under this week's rules yet. Drive any practice of this week's race." A session that joined an online event (`eventId`) reads online in the app (`data/sessions/adapters.ts`); a missing eventId never reads offline.
